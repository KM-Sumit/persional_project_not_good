# Reference apps: Army Study and Winners Institute

Researched 2026-10-21. These are the two apps the owner named as the bar UCI must match or beat.
Sources: Play Store listings with metadata and about 560 reviews per app, scraped with `google-play-scraper` (newest and most helpful), the App Store listing, both institutes' websites and web apps, and the store screenshots, which were inspected visually.
Web content is treated as data.

**Headline:** neither app is custom built. Both are white-label apps from **Appx** (appx.co.in, "My Coaching by Appx"). They share one feature set, one UI template and one set of bugs.
Matching them means matching Appx. Beating them means fixing what reviewers say Appx gets wrong: janky lists, PDFs that will not open, downloads that fail, single-device lockout, Hindi-only tests, too many notifications and weak support.
The audience overlap is real. Winners is in Indore and sells the same MP exams UCI targets (MPPSC, ESB/Vyapam, MP Police, MPSI, MPTET, Patwari).

Legend: **confirmed** means seen on the listing, website or screenshots. **Appx standard** means part of the Appx template that both apps run, or reported by reviewers, but not seen on a screen.

### Student features

- **Home (confirmed from screenshot):** hamburger drawer, logo and a notification bell with an unread badge. Below is a 2-column tile grid in this order: New Courses ("Buy course"), Old Courses, Buy Test Series, Free Test (Weekly), New Books 2026, Free Content, Old Papers (PYQ), Notes & Updates (free notes), Time Table (class schedule), E-Books, Syllabus, Job Alert, Selection (results and toppers), Daily Current Affairs.
- **Bottom tabs (confirmed):** Home · My Courses · Live · Downloads · Chat.
- **Live tab (confirmed):** a list of upcoming and current live classes, each with a thumbnail, title and "Live at: 10-12-2025 at 02:00 pm". The marketing calls out "Classes schedule", "Live classes notifications" and "4K panel live classes".
- **Batches:** paid "Foundation" and "Selection" batches per exam, plus an "All in One Mega Combo Pack". A course page lists admission start, class start, duration, "Validity: till exam", language (Hindi), level and mode. Pricing shows MRP, offer price and a % off badge, with a countdown "Limited time offer" timer ([course page](https://armystudy.in/course/army-gd-tdn-wmp-foundation-batch-2027-live-course/)).
- **Each batch includes:** live classes plus an HD recording of each one, daily PDF class notes, topic-wise practice sets, an All India Ranking Test, topic-wise tests, and a "Doubt Group" run by faculty.
- **Test series** ("Brahmastra", Rs 99 against an MRP of Rs 199): numbered "Ranking Tests", 1 free mock, 2500+ questions, all-India rank, "Have a coupon?" at checkout ([test series page](https://armystudy.in/test-series/army-gd-tdn-wmp-brahmastra-test-series-2027/)).
- **Free funnel content on site and app:** daily and weekly current affairs ("Top 100 Weekly Current Affairs MCQ"), PYQs, sample papers, one-liners, top questions, subject study material and job alerts.
- **Books store:** printed books such as Navy MR Practice Set, Static GK "Rajya Darshan" and model papers. They are sold in the app and on Flipkart.
- **Appx standard:** OTP login with a single-device lock, in-app encrypted video and PDF download for offline use, playback speed and quality controls, live chat, coupons, push notifications, a web app and desktop apps.
- **Language:** content and paid mocks are **Hindi only**. Reviewers ask for an English toggle (see below).
- **Not seen:** dark mode, referral, certificates, gamification (streaks or XP), an in-app doubt-ticket system (the "Doubt Group" is likely Telegram/WhatsApp or Appx chat), and AI.

### Admin features

These come from the Appx console as Appx advertises it ([appx.co.in](https://appx.co.in/)): course, test and e-book builder, low-latency live streaming with chat, DRM content security, 10+ UI themes, coupons, push notifications, memberships and merchandise, website builder, and Mac, Windows, Android and iOS apps.

### What reviewers praise

Out of 558 reviews, 179 positive reviews mention teachers. Reviewers praise the teachers ("Biju sir", "zero se hero"), the low price, "complete preparation" for Agniveer, the notes PDFs, the daily and weekly tests, the All-India mock and its rank, the books, and that tests match the real paper ("25% of my paper came from the test series").

### What reviewers complain about (rated 1 to 3, with helpful votes)

- No download option for classes and download errors. One reviewer asks for "pause while downloading" (96 and 63 votes).
- Hindi only. Reviewers ask for an English or bilingual language toggle on mocks and PDFs: "free tests in English but paid is only Hindi" (29, 43, 11 and 142 votes).
- Login and account lockout: "blocked my account 10-20 minutes" (17 votes).
- PDFs sometimes do not open. PDFs save only inside the app, not to phone storage. There is no pinch-zoom on the screen.
- Heavy use of data and battery. The app asks to be updated every day. One reviewer saw "server under maintenance" right after buying.
- Support is unreachable: "helpline always busy" and "technical team 3/10".
- Promises are not delivered ("50 model sets promised, none") and mock difficulty is uneven ("Airforce mocks too easy").

## 4. Top 15 gaps UCI must close

Ordered by impact on "never lack at any point". Each item fits the locked stack: YouTube, R2, Gemini, Razorpay, PWA.

1. **Live classes plus timetable.** Add a `live_sessions` table (course, title, YouTube Live video ID, `startsAt`). Add a Live tab with Upcoming/Now/Past lists, embed the YouTube Live player, and turn the session into the recorded lesson automatically when the stream ends.
2. **Web push notifications.** Use VAPID web push from the service worker (`web-push` on the server) for live-starting-in-10-min, new lesson, test opening and result reminders. The APK wrapper later reuses this through FCM.
3. **Per-course notification preferences.** Let students mute per course or per category, with a daily digest by default. This answers the "308 unread" spam complaint directly.
4. **Bilingual questions (Hindi/English toggle).** Add `textHi`/`optionsHi` columns to questions and an "EN | हिं" switch in the attempt header that is remembered per user. Gemini translates a module in one batch job that staff review.
5. **Daily current affairs.** A `/app/current-affairs` hub with Daily (YouTube video plus a markdown summary), PDFs (R2) and Monthly (e-magazine PDF) tabs. Gemini drafts the 10 MCQs of the day from the summary.
6. **Daily quiz with a leaderboard.** One 10-question quiz per day per exam, built from the bank. It can reuse the test engine as a `testKind: 'quiz'`, adds a daily and weekly leaderboard cached in Valkey, and feeds the Home card.
7. **Coupons and offer pricing.** A `coupons` table (code, % or flat amount, product scope, max uses, expiry), applied server-side before the Razorpay order is created. Products also get an MRP, a % off badge and an offer-ends countdown.
8. **Doubt solving.** A "Ask a doubt" button on each lesson and each question (text plus photo uploaded to R2). Gemini drafts an answer immediately and a teacher verifies or edits it in a `/admin/doubts` queue. The student is notified by push.
9. **Demo before buying.** Add a `isFree`/preview flag per lesson and per test, and show "Watch demo" and "Try free test" on every paid card and detail page, as Winners' "View Demo" does.
10. **Offline PDFs and notes.** A "Save offline" button caches the R2 signed PDF or notes HTML in the service-worker Cache Storage. Add a Downloads screen with sort options, size used and delete. Downloads survive logout, which fixes both apps' top complaints. Videos stay on YouTube.
11. **Reliable PDF viewer.** An in-app pdf.js viewer with pinch-zoom, page memory and a "Save to device" option (signed R2 URL with content-disposition). This targets the "PDF won't open" and "can't save to phone" complaints.
12. **Exam syllabus and job alerts.** Markdown pages per exam (`exams.syllabusMd`) and a job-alerts list (title, last date, link, PDF on R2) on the public site for SEO, mirrored in the app with push.
13. **Test-taking comfort.** Font-size A-/A+ controls, a sticky answer state that never loses marked answers (already autosaved), section-wise review and topic-wise strength/weakness analysis on the result page.
14. **Player controls.** A custom speed menu (0.75x to 2x) over the YouTube IFrame API that is reachable in fullscreen and portrait. Add resume from the last position (already stored), named chapters (never "Unit 1") and "New" dots on chapters with unseen lessons.
15. **Community and support.** A light course "Posts" feed for teacher announcements with reactions, a "Help" screen with WhatsApp support and a tracked ticket per payment issue, and self-serve receipts and retry for failed Razorpay webhooks. This answers "paid but course not granted".

**Later:** books store with shipping, referral credits, certificates of completion, streaks and goals, audio-only lesson mode (a YouTube audio-only UI that hides the iframe), and content watermarking.

**UCI edges to keep loud in marketing:** multi-device Google sign-in (no lockouts), dark mode, password-protected live tests, markdown notes with good typography, a premium smooth UI (both rivals are called "janky"), AI question generation, and real progress and resume.
